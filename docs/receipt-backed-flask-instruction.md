# Evidence-backed interpersonal flask instruction

Status: first bounded social learning slice of #107 and #110. This is *instruction using a tested object*, not passive observational learning or generic conversation.

A resident with personally witnessed, receipt-backed **successful flask-method evidence** and one actually carried flask **made by that resident** may offer an eight-minute `teach_flask` action to a nearby, interested, idle resident who does not know the method. The instructor must hold the example, both residents must remain at the same world level and within two tiles at completion, and the listener must still be available and interested. Instruction is a practical deliberate action. No words in `socialize`, model output, physical proximity alone, found flask, or bare `knowledge.recipes` flag can authorize it.

The authoritative `teach_flask` world operation awards the *learner*, not the speaker, the deliberately taught procedure. It produces **no materials** and makes no copies of the demonstration flask. `WorldReceipts` includes changes in each affected other actor's recipe set within `delta.otherActors[].knowledge.learnedMethods`. After the receipt is committed, `EvidenceMemory.fromInstruction` creates one learner-private `interaction` episode (certainty `reported`, not `observed`) and one method source record with `provenance: instruction`, `taughtBy`, and the receipt ID. The original speaker retains firsthand evidence and the demonstration object. Save/load preserves both knowledge and its teaching provenance. Late, duplicate, forged or misdirected evidence calls cannot confer the method.

The learner can follow the taught flask recipe when they later obtain real wood, a manufactured binding, a cutting blade, and carrying capacity. They still have to produce a physical result with the ordinary timed world executor; a successful lesson is not proof that the learner has personally fabricated a working vessel.

**Validation:** `tests/flask-instruction.test.mjs` compares actual teaching to an unsupported recipe flag, carried product alone and social contact, checks time and coexistence, verifies private receipt delta, no material duplication, one-time instruction, interruption by participant departure, disinterest/busy status, and checkpoint restoration.

**Remaining scope:** teaching does not yet handle partial understanding, disputes, multiple steps observed in place, learner skill-dependent comprehension, failed demonstrations, or other manufacturing categories. Real peer conversation has a separate dialogue channel, but freeform claims still do not grant technique knowledge. Social transfer must be extended through evidence-provenanced procedures rather than made global to all recipe catalog entries.
